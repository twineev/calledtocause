Every founder has an origin story. Every investor has a reason they said yes. Called to Cause is the show where we get both.

We sit down with the builders creating category-defining companies and the investors putting their capital where their conviction is. No pitch decks, no polish. Just the real moments: the years nobody saw, the check that almost didn't get written, the belief that outlasted the doubt.

This is our introduction to the show: what it is, why we started it, and what we want out of it.

## What Called to Cause is

Called to Cause is a conversation series for founders and investors who are building and backing companies that matter. It is hosted by us, Twinee Madan and Valeria Mushak, and every episode is a long, unhurried conversation rather than a highlight reel.

Founders talk about what it actually costs to build: the trade-offs, the pivots, the thing they got wrong before they got it right. Investors talk about why they said yes when everyone else said no, and what conviction looks like before the traction does.

## Same story, both sides of the table

Capital and company-building are usually told separately. Founders get one set of stories, investors get another, and the people in the middle are left to guess how the two connect. We think they are the same story.

We have both spent our careers close to it. Twinee came up through events, customer success and go-to-market at early-stage startups, and learned there how startups really run. Together, we are building Angel Activists, a platform for angel investors and the founders they back. Founders and investors, whether angels, venture capitalists or family offices, are two sides of one relationship, and most of what goes wrong between them comes down to never having heard how the other side thinks.

Called to Cause started there. We wanted to put both sides in the same room, on the record, and let the people who have lived it do the talking.

## Why we started it

Three things kept coming up.

- **Startup stories are too clean.** By the time a company makes the news, the hard years have been edited out. The fundraising that stalled, the hire that did not work, the quiet stretch before anything took off: that is where the useful lessons are, and almost nobody tells them.
- **Investing is a black box to the people who need it most.** A lot of founders do not know how an investor decides, and a lot of new investors do not know how to start. Hearing an investor explain a real decision, in their own words, does more than any playbook.
- **Better capital should reach more builders.** We care about founders and investors who are working on things that matter, and about opening doors for people the traditional networks tend to skip.

## What we want out of it

We want this to be useful, not just interesting. Specifically:

- **A library founders can learn from.** Honest accounts of how companies get built, so the next founder does not have to figure it all out alone.
- **A window into how investors think.** Angels, venture capitalists, family offices and operators-turned-investors explaining what they look for and what they have gotten wrong, so founders know what to expect and more people feel ready to write their first check.
- **Real connections.** Some of the best things to come out of a good conversation happen after it ends. We hope guests meet each other, and that founders, investors and operators in the audience find the people they have been looking for.
- **A community around the work.** Founders, angels, investors and operators who want to build and back things with purpose, and who are willing to be candid about how hard that is.
- **To keep learning ourselves.** Every guest teaches us something. We plan to share what we take away, along with the interviews.

## What you will find here

New episodes are on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@calledtocause) and Spotify, and we publish a written companion for each conversation on [Substack](https://calledtocause.substack.com). On this site you can watch the full interviews, read the write-ups, and see what we are working on next.

If you are a founder or operator with a story worth telling, or an investor with a decision you still think about, we would love to hear from you.

Because the best companies aren't just funded; they're called into being.
